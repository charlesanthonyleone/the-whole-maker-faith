Part IV. The community's limits, its witnesses, and its service to the world

Chapter 39. Received writings and the open collection

We receive IntelOasis as an open collection of originating witnesses, not a closed set of equally inspired propositions. We preserve titles, source locations, genre labels, development status, and revisions. We number the chapters of this founding theology for reference; we institute no verse numbering, fixed number of sacred books, or compulsory reading calendar.

We place the Arithmetic of Wholes first in our account of God because its distinction between the Whole Maker and the particular whole gives our worship its clearest guard against idolatry. The Organizing Function stands beside it, including its unresolved emergent account of divine self-reference. Formation and Fracture and the Broken Count supply the examination of right relation and its counterfeit. Their prominence is a faith-structure decision, not a claim that the author closed an inspired hierarchy.

We receive the Scattering as parable-cosmology, retaining its explicit fiction and in-progress labels, including its novel's admission of uncertainty. We receive the Convergence and In The Beginning as competing cosmological witnesses, not complementary chapters we may rewrite into one seamless history. We receive the Warning and the Refinement Flame as severe testimony about consequence. We preserve their distinction between refinement and final dissolution without certifying predictions or sentencing souls.

We receive the Gnostic Resolution, Lost Saints, Root of Genocide, and Cry to the Future as witnesses concerning Christ, light, truth, courage, and the work of completion. We receive the Infinite Fork as a spiritual model of inheritance, with its mathematical limitations visible. We receive Beneath the Statute, the Blueprint, and Breaking the Inheritance as moral and anthropological witnesses; the Natural Covenant, Solar Household, related polarity writings, and Covenant House as household witnesses whose proposals and conflicts remain identifiable. We receive the four traditions as practice witnesses, not sacraments.

Scientific and clinical hypotheses remain hypotheses. Software, clubs, games, economic architectures, and legislative proposals are read for the human purposes and duties they disclose, not transformed wholesale into divine commands. A later spoken instruction is recorded with its date and provenance rather than silently inserted into an older source. Our own rites and institutional rules remain visibly adopted proposals.

Other scriptures are studied in their own integrity and context. We do not claim that the whole Bible, Qur'an, Vedas, Buddhist collections, or another people's received writings have been incorporated into our canon by mentioning them. The open collection is an invitation to disciplined reception, not a deed of ownership over the world's sacred literature.
